The Mus'haf, as printed

All 604 pages of the Madinah Mus'haf, the same words on the same 15 lines, turned right to left. Two pages open side by side on a tablet.

Lit, not just coloured

Thirty palettes: luminous inks like Iris, Ember and Aurora that shimmer with their own light, calm daylight papers, and the classics.

Recitation, word by word

Around a hundred reciters. The verse lights as it is recited, and with quran.com's voices every word does too. Repeat, echo, speed, sleep timer.

Translation anywhere

Beneath each verse, side by side, or word by word. Turn a Mus'haf page over to read its translation, or hold a verse to peek through the lens.

Tafsir and tajweed

Ibn Kathir, Al-Muyassar, As-Sa'di, At-Tabari, Al-Qurtubi and more. Tajweed colours drawn on the Mus'haf's own script.

Khatmah and hifz

Plan a khatmah and see today's portion. Memorise with a cover you slide down line by line, hidden words, and reviews that come back when due.
