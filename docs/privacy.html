Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Mishkat ("the app") is a Quran reader made by Mulhimfy. It is built so that you can read, listen and study without giving anything of yourself away. This page explains, plainly, what the app does with data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information, and we have no servers that receive it.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings: palette, reading view, sizes, language, reciter, translations.
  • Your place in the Mus'haf, bookmarks, colours and notes.
  • Your reading journey, khatmah plan and memorisation reviews.
  • Recitations, translations and tafsir you download for offline use.

These live only in the app's storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device's settings, deletes them all.

Content the app downloads

To play recitations and show translations and tafsir that are not bundled, the app requests them from public Quran services: quran.com and Quranic Audio (api.quran.com, api.qurancdn.com, verses.quran.com, audio.qurancdn.com, download.quranicaudio.com), EveryAyah (everyayah.com), mp3quran (mp3quran.net), Tarteel (audio-cdn.tarteel.ai) and King Saud University (quran.ksu.edu.sa). As with any download, these services see a standard request (your IP address and the app's request); the app sends them no personal information. Their own privacy policies apply to their servers.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules it on your device. Nothing about it leaves your phone.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: ahmeddevyfy@gmail.com.

ما الذي نجمعه؟

لا شيء. لا حسابات ولا تسجيل دخول ولا إعلانات ولا تحليلات ولا تتبّع. لا نجمع بيانات شخصية ولا نخزّنها ولا نبيعها ولا نشاركها، وليست لدينا خوادم تستقبلها.

ما الذي يبقى على جهازك؟

إعداداتك، وموضعك في المصحف، وعلاماتك وألوانك وملاحظاتك، ورحلة قراءتك وخطة الختمة ومراجعات الحفظ، وما تنزّله من تلاوات وترجمات وتفاسير. كل ذلك يبقى في ذاكرة التطبيق على جهازك ولا يُرسل إلى أي مكان، ويُحذف كله بحذف التطبيق أو مسح بياناته.

المحتوى الذي ينزّله التطبيق

لتشغيل التلاوات وعرض الترجمات والتفاسير غير المضمّنة، يطلبها التطبيق من خدمات قرآنية عامة (quran.com وQuranic Audio وEveryAyah وmp3quran وTarteel وجامعة الملك سعود). ترى هذه الخدمات طلبًا عاديًا كأي تنزيل (عنوان IP)، ولا يرسل التطبيق إليها أي بيانات شخصية.
