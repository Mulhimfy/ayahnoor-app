How do I…

See the controls? Tap the page. Tap again to read without them.

Read a verse's translation in the Mus'haf? Hold your finger on it: its translation appears, and follows your finger from verse to verse. Let go for play, bookmark, share and more. Or turn the whole page over with the translation button at the top.

Learn a word? Double-tap it on the Mus'haf page (or tap it in the Verses view) for its meaning, transliteration and sound.

Listen from a verse? Hold the verse, then Play. In Listen you choose the reciter, how far to go, repeats, echo, speed and a sleep timer.

Listen offline? More → Downloads, or the download line at the foot of Listen, keeps a surah (or the whole Quran) for the chosen reciter.

Memorise? More → Memorize → Hidden. In the Mus'haf a cover slides over the page: drag or tap it down line by line as you recite. In the Verses view the words hide until you tap them.

Plan a khatmah? More → Khatmah: choose how many days, and the app shows today's portion and keeps count as you read.
